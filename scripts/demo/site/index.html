AI meeting notes your whole team can trust

Quillpad is an AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes and summarizes video calls, then turns every decision into an action item with an owner and a due date. It works with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams and supports 30 languages.

What Quillpad does

  • Live transcription with speaker labels and 95% word accuracy on clear audio.
  • Summaries in five formats: overview, decisions, action items, risks and follow-up email.
  • Action items pushed to your task tracker and chat workspace.
  • Search across every meeting your team has recorded.

Who uses Quillpad

More than 4,000 remote and hybrid teams use Quillpad, from five-person startups to sales organizations with 300 account executives. Teams report saving about 4 hours per person each week on note taking and follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

Is Quillpad free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 10 recorded meetings per month. Paid plans start at $12 per user per month.

Does Quillpad store my recordings?

Recordings are encrypted at rest and deleted after 90 days by default. Admins can shorten retention or turn recording off and keep transcripts only.
