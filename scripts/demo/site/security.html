Security and privacy

Quillpad is built for teams that discuss customers, contracts and hiring in their meetings. This page explains how that data is protected.

Encryption

Recordings and transcripts are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2 or higher in transit.

Data residency

Business customers choose whether their data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Oregon).

Retention

Recordings are deleted after 90 days by default. Admins can set any period from 1 to 365 days, or keep transcripts only.

Compliance

Quillpad completed a SOC 2 Type II audit in 2026 and offers a Data Processing Agreement for GDPR.
