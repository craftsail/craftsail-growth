Features

Quillpad covers the full meeting loop: it joins the call, writes the notes, assigns the follow-ups and makes every conversation searchable later.

Transcription

Speaker-labelled transcripts in 30 languages, ready two minutes after the call ends. Custom vocabulary keeps product names and acronyms spelled correctly.

Summaries

Choose a template per meeting type: sales discovery, sprint planning, one-on-one, interview or board meeting. Each template decides which sections the summary contains.

Action items

Quillpad detects commitments such as "I'll send the proposal by Friday", assigns an owner and a due date, and syncs them to your task tracker.

Meeting search

Ask a question in plain language, for example "what did we decide about pricing in March?", and get the answer with links to the exact moments in each recording.

How it works

  1. Connect your calendar.
  2. Quillpad joins scheduled calls as a participant, or you upload a recording.
  3. Review the summary, edit if needed and share it with one click.
