How to choose an AI meeting notes app in 2026

An AI meeting notes app records a call, transcribes it and writes a summary with decisions and action items. The good ones save each attendee three to five hours a week; the wrong one creates notes nobody trusts.

1. Check accuracy on your own calls

Vendors quote accuracy on clean studio audio. Record three real meetings with accents, crosstalk and product jargon, run them through each tool and count the errors in names and numbers.

2. Match the meeting platforms you use

Most tools support Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. Check whether the assistant joins as a bot, records locally or needs an upload, because this changes what guests see.

3. Read the privacy terms

Look for encryption at rest, a retention setting, EU data residency if you need it, and a Data Processing Agreement. Ask whether recordings are used to train models.

4. Compare the real price

CriterionWhat to look for
Free planAt least 10 meetings per month
Per-seat price$10 to $25 per user per month is typical
LanguagesEvery language your customers speak
RetentionConfigurable, with transcript-only mode

Summary

Shortlist two or three tools, test them on real meetings for two weeks and pick the one whose action items your team actually follows up on.
